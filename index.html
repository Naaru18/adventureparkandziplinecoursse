Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our zipline and adventure experiences

Is zipline safe for beginners? ▲

Yes, our zipline experiences are designed for all skill levels. We use certified safety equipment and provide trained guides to ensure a secure and enjoyable ride.

What is the minimum age for adventure activities? ▼

Most activities are suitable for participants aged 10 and above. Some advanced courses may have additional height and weight requirements.

Do I need prior experience? ▼

No prior experience is required. Our team provides complete instructions and safety briefings before every activity.

Are the activities eco-friendly? ▼

Yes, EcoZip Adventures follows eco-friendly practices and ensures minimal environmental impact while delivering thrilling experiences.

Can I book group or corporate events? ▼

Absolutely! We offer customized group packages for schools, corporates, and events with special discounts and guided sessions.
